## Chapter 1: Littleroot Town → Route 101

```

  1. Mudkip ♂ (Lv. 5) - HP: [||||||||||] 100% | Status: OK
```

**Auto-pick [1]: Charge in.** Zigzagoon (Lv. 2) lunges at Professor Birch.
- Mudkip used **Tackle**! Hit. Zigzagoon HP 55%.
- Zigzagoon used **Growl**. Mudkip's Attack fell.
- Mudkip used **Tackle**! Zigzagoon fainted. Mudkip gained 14 EXP.

Birch, dusting himself off: "You handled that like a natural! Keep Mudkip. It clearly chose you."
He sends you to Route 103 to find his daughter **May**, who challenges you on the spot.

**Rival Battle: May** (Treecko Lv. 5)
- Treecko has the type edge on Mudkip. Auto-pick: wear it down with **Tackle**, **Growl** to blunt Pound.
- Mudkip HP drops to 30%, then Tackle lands a critical hit. Treecko fainted.
- **Mudkip grew to Lv. 6!** Learned **Mud-Slap**. Prize: ₽300.

Birch gives you the **Pokédex** and 5 **Poké Balls**.
