02 / KEEP YOUR PROGRESS

Your adventure, saved.

Cartridge save: use the game’s own SAVE menu. Give it a few seconds to finish before closing.

Save state: use the emulator toolbar to download a snapshot, then upload it to continue later. Keep a backup outside the browser.

Same browser, same address: browser saves belong to 127.0.0.1:8743. Switching browsers or clearing site data won’t bring your saves along.

Story save: use Export / Import in Story mode. These JSON files are separate from your GBA saves.

Loading a state replaces the current game session. Save your current progress first.
